Traditional Interior Design

Traditional Indian interiors bring warmth, craftsmanship and cultural character into the home through woodwork, textiles, colour, patterns and timeless decorative details.

AR INFRACON DESIGN GUIDE

Heritage, Warmth & Character

AR Infracon can reinterpret traditional Indian elements for contemporary homes. Carved wood, warm lighting, patterned fabrics, brass accents, handcrafted décor and rich earthy colours can be combined with modern comfort so the space feels rooted without feeling outdated.

THE AR INFRACON APPROACH

Designed Around Your Lifestyle

Every project starts with understanding the space, your requirements, preferred look and practical needs. We then develop the design, materials, lighting, furniture and execution details as one complete interior solution.

Our objective is to create a finished interior that looks beautiful in photographs but, more importantly, works comfortably every day.

WHERE IT WORKS BEST

DESIGN ELEMENTS

What Defines This Style?

Key elements our designers consider while developing this look.

Crafted Woodwork

Wooden panels, carved details and furniture can introduce warmth and heritage.

Rich Textures

Textiles, rugs, cushions and wall finishes add depth and character.

Warm Lighting

Layered warm lighting enhances wood, fabrics and decorative details.

Brass & Metal

Brass accents and handcrafted accessories provide traditional character.

Indian Colours

Terracotta, cream, maroon, green and earthy shades can create a welcoming palette.

Modern Comfort

Traditional character is balanced with contemporary furniture, storage and functionality.
