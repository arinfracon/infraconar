ABOUT AR INFRACON

Creating Spaces That Inspire

More than interiors, we create experiences designed around you.

AR Infracon is an interior design and execution company focused on creating beautiful, functional and comfortable spaces.

We believe every interior should reflect the personality, lifestyle and vision of the people who use it.

Our team combines creative design thinking with practical planning to deliver interiors that are both attractive and useful.

From concept development and space planning to materials, furniture and final execution, we take care of every important detail.

We work with trusted materials and experienced professionals to maintain quality throughout the project.

With transparent communication and a customer-first approach, we keep the design and execution process simple and stress-free.

Our goal is simple — to turn your ideas into interiors that you will love living and working in for years to come.

Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions about our interior design process.

How long does an interior project take?

Project duration depends on the size, design requirements, materials and scope of work. After understanding your project, our team can provide an estimated timeline.

Do you provide 3D designs?

Yes. We provide 3D visualizations to help you understand the proposed design, colours, furniture and overall appearance before execution.

Is the consultation free?

Yes. You can contact our team for an initial consultation and discuss your requirements, budget and preferred design style.

Can I choose my own materials and colours?

Yes. We can help you select materials, finishes, colours, furniture and other elements according to your preferences and project requirements.

Do you provide a warranty?

Warranty and service terms depend on the selected products, materials and project package. Our team will explain the applicable terms before execution.
